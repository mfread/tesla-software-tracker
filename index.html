About this data

Data courtesy Teslascope, Tessie, Not a Tesla App, TeslaFi, TeslAnt and X. Fleet labels such as “phasing out” are global tracker status. A HW tag next to a firmware version means that version has been observed on that Tesla car computer and not that every car globally with that computer has received that firmware.
