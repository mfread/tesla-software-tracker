About this data

Data courtesy Teslascope, Tessie, Not a Tesla App, TeslaFi, TeslAnt and X. Current year (n) and previous year (n−1) are live overnight snapshots. Older years are locked archives reconstructed from public trackers — not nightly scrapes. A HW tag on an archive/car-only leaf is from saved cars or that archive file, not a claim that every car with that computer received it. Fleet labels such as “phasing out” are global tracker status.
